About this reconstruction

This interactive 3D model shows a coral reef patch captured using photogrammetry and processed with Gaussian splatting technology. The reconstruction allows you to explore the reef structure from any angle, providing a detailed view of the coral formations and marine environment.

Use the embedded viewer above, or click the button to open in a new tab for the best experience. The 3D viewer works best on desktop computers with a modern browser. Mobile users may prefer the video walkthrough.
