Click and hold left mouse button to rotate, right mouse button to pan, scroll to zoom, double click to select focal point.

This demo shows the aftermath from wildfires in 2020 on two Sequoia trees in the Big Basin Redwoods State Park in California. The tallest of the two trees is at least 50m in height. This Gaussian splat was trained in minutes using 460 drone images.

Data courtesy of: Auto Tree (2023) Photogrammetry. Distributed by Open Heritage 3D. https://doi.org/10.26301/c2ys-a430. Splat trained using Lichtfeld Studio, thank you to the developers!
